PIZZA-DIP

Servings: 8 servings

Author: Whitney Bond

Are you looking for the ultimate crowd-pleasing snack
for your next party, game day, or casual get-together? Look no further. This hot, bubbly Skillet Pizza Dip is the definitive answer to your hosting prayers. It delivers all the comforting, savory flavors of a classic pepperoni pizza but in a much more shareable, interactive format. Every scoop is packed with a rich cream cheese base, tangy marinara, and gooey melted cheese that pairs flawlessly with golden, garlic-buttered pull-apart bread.
Pro-Tip for the Perfect Cheese Pull:
If you want that picture-perfect, mouth-watering cheese stretch, skip the pre-shredded cheese from the grocery store aisle. Bagged shredded cheese is coated with anti-caking agents (like potato starch or cellulose) to keep it from clumping, which unfortunately also stops it from melting smoothly. Instead, grab a solid block of low-moisture mozzarella and grate it yourself. Taking those two extra minutes to shred your own cheese guarantees a beautifully smooth, undeniably gooey, and perfectly stretchy dip every single time!

Part1: 🛒 Ingredients

🧀THE PULL-APART BREAD

  • 16.3 ounce can Grands biscuit dough, 8 count
  • ¼ cup melted butter
  • 2 teaspoons parmesan cheese, grated
  • 1 teaspoon Italian seasoning, click link for the recipe

🍞THE PIZZA DIP

  • 8 ounces cream cheese, softened
  • 1 cup ricotta cheese
  • 3 teaspoons Italian seasoning, divided
  • 1 cup marinara sauce, or pizza sauce
  • 1 ½ cups mozzarella cheese, grated
  • 8 slices pepperoni, quartered (optional topping)

Part2:👩‍🍳Instructions

Prep the Oven and Bread

--
  • Step 1 Preheat the oven to 350°F.
    • --
  • Step 2 Cut each biscuit in half, then roll each half of dough into a ball.
    • --
  • Step 3 Combine the melted butter, parmesan cheese, and 1 teaspoon Italian seasoning in a bowl.
    • --
  • Step 4 Roll each of the balls of biscuit dough in the seasoned butter, then arrange them around the edge of a greased large cast iron skillet.

    • Prep the Dip

    --
  • Step 1 In a large bowl, use an electric mixer, or stand mixer, to combine the cream cheese, ricotta cheese and 2 teaspoons Italian seasoning.
    • --
  • Step 2 Spread the cream cheese mixture in the bottom of the skillet in the middle of the balls of dough.
    • --
  • Step 3 Top with the marinara sauce, shredded mozzarella cheese and remaining 1 teaspoon Italian seasoning. Top with pepperonis, or another pizza topping of your choice.
    • --
  • Step 4 Place in the oven and bake for 25-30 minutes.
